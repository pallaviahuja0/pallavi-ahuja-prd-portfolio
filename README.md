## 📐 PRD Structure

Each PRD follows a consistent structure:

```
1. Problem Statement       — What problem are we solving and for whom?
2. Goals & Objectives      — Primary goal, business goals, product goals
3. Non-Goals               — Explicit scope boundaries
4. Background & Context    — Market, competitive, and strategic context
5. Target Users & Personas — Who uses this product and what do they need?
6. Scope                   — What's in and out, by release/phase
7. User Stories            — Epics and acceptance criteria
8. Functional Requirements — Prioritised requirements table (P0/P1/P2)
9. Success Metrics & KPIs  — Primary, secondary, and guardrail metrics
10. Product Roadmap        — Release/phase timeline
11. Risks & Mitigations    — RAID log with likelihood and impact
12. Dependencies           — Cross-team and external dependencies
13. Open Questions         — Unresolved decisions with owners
```
